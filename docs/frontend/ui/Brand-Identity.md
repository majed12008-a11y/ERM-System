# Brand Identity — National Research Ethics Management System (NERMS)

| Language | Name |
|----------|------|
| العربية | النظام الوطني لإدارة الموافقات الأخلاقية للأبحاث والدراسات |
| English | National Research Ethics Management System |
| Official Abbreviation | **NERMS** |

---

## Identity Message

NERMS is the national platform for the governance and management of research ethics approvals in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, under the supervision of the Ministry of Health.

The identity must reflect:

| Value | Manifestation |
|-------|--------------|
| **Trust** | Professional, stable, reliable interface — the user should feel confident in the system |
| **Governance** | Clear hierarchy, structured workflows, transparent status tracking |
| **Ethics** | Human-centric design that respects the sensitivity of research involving human participants |
| **Transparency** | Visible audit trail, clear application status, open communication channels |
| **Participant Protection** | Safety-first design language — shields, checkmarks, secure indicators |
| **Scientific Research** | Academic rigor reflected in clean, data-rich interfaces |
| **Ministry of Health** | Official government identity — national emblem, institutional branding |

## Brand Personality

NERMS should feel like:

- **Authoritative** but not intimidating — a government system that is professional yet approachable
- **Modern** but not trendy — timeless design that will age well over years of use
- **Scientific** but not cold — data-rich interfaces with human warmth
- **Secure** but not locked down — visible security indicators without friction

---

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over creativity** — every design decision must prioritize legibility and understanding
2. **Consistency above all** — one component, one way to use it
3. **Accessibility is not optional** — WCAG AA minimum, AAA where practical
4. **RTL-first** — design for Arabic as the primary language, English as secondary
5. **Performance matters** — lightweight SVGs, system fonts, minimal JavaScript
6. **Government-grade** — institutional look that inspires trust, not a startup aesthetic

## Target Audience

| Group | Needs |
|-------|-------|
| **Researchers / Applicants** | Submit applications, upload documents, track review status |
| **Reviewers / Committee Members** | Review applications, vote, provide feedback |
| **Ethics Committee Administrators** | Manage workflows, schedule meetings, generate reports |
