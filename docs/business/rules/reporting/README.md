# Reporting Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that govern report inclusion, aggregation, interpretation, periods, and authorized reporting audiences.

## Scope
Reporting rules for operational reporting, executive reporting, oversight analytics, performance monitoring, and cross-institution reporting.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate reporting rule files using the assigned reporting number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Calculation, security, or compliance rules whose primary purpose is not reporting.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with reporting owners and governance authorities.
