# National Policy Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that derive from national governance direction, ministry authority, or national research ethics policy.

## Scope
National policy rules for national adoption, oversight, governance consistency, regulatory alignment, and national reporting expectations.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate national policy rule files using the assigned national policy number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Institution-only local rules or rules without national policy authority as the primary source.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with national policy authority.
