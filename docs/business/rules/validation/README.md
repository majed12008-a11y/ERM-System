# Validation Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that determine whether business information satisfies completeness, consistency, or readiness expectations.

## Scope
Validation rules for submissions, evidence, declarations, records, review inputs, and reporting inputs.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate validation rule files using the assigned validation number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Eligibility, workflow, decision, calculation, or reporting rules whose primary purpose is not validation.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with process owners and quality reviewers.
