# Committee Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that govern research ethics committee authority, responsibilities, participation, deliberation, and decision governance.

## Scope
Committee rules for reviewer participation, committee decisions, committee roles, deliberation expectations, and committee governance responsibilities.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate committee rule files using the assigned committee number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Administrative, workflow, notification, or security rules whose primary purpose is not committee governance.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with Research Ethics Committee leadership.
