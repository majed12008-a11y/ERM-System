# Calculation Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that govern calculated business values, counts, scores, durations, thresholds, and indicators.

## Scope
Calculation rules for reporting measures, maturity measures, deadlines, risk indicators, and business metrics.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate calculation rule files using the assigned calculation number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Reporting rules or implementation formulas whose primary purpose is not business calculation meaning.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with business owners and reporting owners.
