# Rule Change Process

## Purpose

This document defines how future business rule changes SHALL be managed.

## Change Steps

1. Identify the rule ID and current version.
2. Describe the proposed change.
3. Identify the business rationale.
4. Identify affected processes, capabilities, entities, and dependent rules.
5. Assess verification impact.
6. Submit for review.
7. Obtain approval from the proper authority.
8. Update status, version, and traceability.

Approved rule meaning SHALL NOT be silently changed.
