# Rule Numbering

## Purpose

This document reserves numbering ranges for future ERM-System business rules.

## Standard

Business rule identifiers SHALL use the format `BR-NNNNNN`.

Identifiers SHALL be unique and SHALL NOT be reused.

Withdrawn identifiers SHOULD remain reserved.
