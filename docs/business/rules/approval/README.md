# Approval Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that govern approval eligibility, approval terms, approval outcomes, and approval representation.

## Scope
Approval rules for approvals, certificates, amendments, closure eligibility, and approval-related conditions.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate approval rule files using the assigned approval number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
General decision, workflow, retention, or notification rules whose primary purpose is not approval governance.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with Research Ethics Governance.
