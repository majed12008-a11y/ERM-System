# Rule Review Process

## Purpose

This document defines the review expectations for future business rules.

## Review Steps

1. Confirm the rule uses the approved template.
2. Confirm the rule has a valid identifier.
3. Confirm the classification and folder placement.
4. Confirm the business owner and business source.
5. Confirm traceability to related processes, capabilities, and domain entities.
6. Confirm dependency and conflict review.
7. Confirm verification method.
8. Confirm approval authority.

## Review Responsibility

Business Rules Architecture coordinates review. Business owners, domain reviewers, process reviewers, security reviewers, and quality reviewers participate where applicable.
