# Draft Rules

## Purpose
Store future draft rule records that are not yet approved.

## Scope
Draft rules under development, review preparation, or source clarification.

## What Belongs Here
Draft rule records with assigned or candidate identifiers.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Approved rules, archived rules, deprecated rules, or uncontrolled notes.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md` when assigned; use a clearly marked candidate name when no ID has been assigned.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates draft review with prospective business owners.
