# Eligibility Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that determine whether a business subject qualifies for an action, state, pathway, role, or responsibility.

## Scope
Eligibility rules for applicants, reviewers, committees, institutions, applications, research activities, and governance pathways.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate eligibility rule files using the assigned eligibility number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Validation, workflow, approval, security, or reporting rules whose primary purpose is not eligibility.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with the relevant business owner.
