# Scheduling Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that govern due dates, review windows, reminders, recurrence, escalation timing, and meeting timing.

## Scope
Scheduling rules for screening, review, conditions, continuing review, reporting, safety events, and committee cycles.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate scheduling rule files using the assigned scheduling number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Workflow, notification, or compliance rules whose primary purpose is not timing.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with process owners.
