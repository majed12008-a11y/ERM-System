# Decision Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that determine business outcomes from approved criteria, facts, or governance judgments.

## Scope
Decision rules for screening outcomes, review outcomes, committee decisions, evidence outcomes, and governance determinations.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate decision rule files using the assigned decision number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Approval, committee, calculation, or reporting rules whose primary purpose is not decision determination.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with the relevant decision authority.
