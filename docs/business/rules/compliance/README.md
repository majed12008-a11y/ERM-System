# Compliance Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that govern alignment with approved governance expectations and compliance obligations.

## Scope
Compliance rules for ethics governance obligations, oversight findings, exceptions, and follow-up requirements.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate compliance rule files using the assigned compliance number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Regulatory, audit, security, or retention rules whose primary purpose is not compliance alignment.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with Research Governance Authority.
