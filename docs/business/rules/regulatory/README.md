# Regulatory Rules

## Purpose
Store future rules that derive from regulatory, statutory, or formally delegated regulatory authority.

## Scope
Regulatory rules for obligations, evidence, reporting, review, approval, retention, or oversight required by regulatory authority.

## What Belongs Here
Approved or candidate regulatory rule files using the assigned regulatory number range.

## What Does NOT Belong Here
Internal policy or compliance rules without regulatory authority as the primary source.

## Naming Convention
Use `BR-NNNNNN-short-rule-name.md`.

## Review Responsibility
Business Rules Architecture coordinates review with regulatory or delegated governance authority.
